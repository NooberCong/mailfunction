Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

mailfunction is a desktop mail client that runs on your computer. It has no server, so the developer never receives your mail, your Google account data or your sign-in details.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests access to your Gmail mailbox and your email address. It uses this only to download your mail and show it to you. It currently reads mail and does not change your mailbox.

Where your data is kept

  • Downloaded mail is stored in a local database on your computer.
  • Your sign-in token is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your computer.

What leaves your computer

The app connects only to Google, to sign you in and to download your mail. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking. Remote images in messages are blocked, so opening a message does not tell the sender you read it. Links you choose to open go to your web browser.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

mailfunction's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Removing an account in the app deletes its local mail and its saved sign-in. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project's GitHub issues.
